Questions before you visit

How do I find a room?

Use Get Invited. You will see nearby chapters, meeting times, and who to write. Visit first — membership comes after the room feels right.

How does a seat work?

Each chapter holds one person per craft. If your category is open, you apply with the membership circle. Interview, references, then a vote.

Do I need a sponsor?

No. After you join, a mentor walks you through the agenda, 1-to-1s, and how to brief the room so members can actually send you work.

Can I join online?

Yes. Alivio runs studio, hybrid, and fully online chapters with the same timed agenda — intros, education, and warm handoffs.
