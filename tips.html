Networking Tips

Practical moves you can use in this week’s Alivio meeting.

1. Ask for a category, not “anyone”

“Operations directors at mid-size logistics firms” is an ask. “Anyone who needs marketing” is noise.

2. Follow up before the next sunrise

Intros die in silence. Send a thank-you, a calendar link, and one useful note.

3. Teach the room your trigger

Give the phrases they will hear at dinner. Make yourself easy to send.

4. Guard the chair

Filling seats fast costs everyone. A weak operator in your category poisons the pipeline.
