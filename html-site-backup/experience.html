FAQ

Straight answers.

How do I find a chapter?

Use Find a Chapter. Filter by city, meeting mode, and your profession. You will see open seats, average deal size, and the next meeting before you visit.

Is the visitor meeting free?

Yes. Your first two visits are free. You apply only if the room fits and your category is open.

What if my profession is already taken?

You can visit nearby chapters, join a forming chapter, or start a new room. We never double a category inside one chapter.

Do I need a sponsor?

No. After two visits you apply online. The membership circle interviews you and checks two professional references.

What does the seat cost?

Metro seats are ₹48,000 a year, growth cities ₹36,000, online chapters ₹28,000. Visitor meetings stay free. Run the ROI calculator on the home page.

How do you keep members reputable?

Application, interview, references, and a 90-day review. Attendance and closed-business numbers are visible to the chapter.
