How to read an open seat

Chapter desk · Mumbai · 4 October 2026

An empty photographer seat in Bandra is not the same as eleven open seats in a new online room. Look at closed YTD, average deal, and 12-week attendance before you claim it.

Three checks

  • Is the complementary craft already in the room? A CA without a realtor closes slower.
  • Does the average deal match your ticket size?
  • Are members actually there on Wednesday at 7:30?

If your category is taken, visit anyway or plant the next room. We never double a craft inside one chapter.
