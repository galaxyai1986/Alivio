Mission

Help members grow through introductions they can trust — and numbers they can defend.

We run local chapters with a global spine. One person per profession in each room. A timed weekly agenda. Referrals that are written down. Leadership that rotates. No politics in the room, no doubled categories, no hidden dues.

How we behave

Four rules. Everything else is logistics.

Give first

The fastest way to earn trust is to send a qualified introduction before you ask for one.

One seat, one craft

Every chapter holds a single exclusive seat per profession. Your category is yours to own.

Show the numbers

Referrals, closed deals, and meeting attendance are public inside the chapter. No vanity metrics.

Keep the promise

Weekly presence, honest follow-up, and clean handoffs. Accountability is the product.

Leadership

Operators, not a personality cult.

Nexora is led by chapter presidents who still sit in the room. Regional directors coach forming chapters. Product lives in Bengaluru. HQ is Mumbai.

We will not put a founder’s face on every page. The chapter is the product.
