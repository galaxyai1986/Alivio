The 60-second that books meetings

Lina D’Souza · Goa · 12 October 2026

Members used to ramble. Name, company, a list of services, a joke. Nobody remembered the line. The room needs a sentence they can repeat to a client at lunch.

The shape

  • Who you help — one craft, one buyer.
  • The problem they already feel.
  • The handoff — what a good referral sounds like.

Example: “I brand founder-led studios in Goa that have outgrown Instagram. A good intro is a hotel or clinic that just hired a marketing intern and still looks homemade.”

Practice it weekly. Feature presentations are ten minutes; the 60-second is the muscle. Clients now repeat my line back to me.
