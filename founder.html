Our Founder

Alivio began with a simple belief: business grows fastest when people give first.

Lena Alivio

After years of watching owners freeze in noisy mixers, Lena designed a weekly table with exclusive seats, a timed agenda, and a rule: the introducer stays on the thread.

The first Alivio room opened in Austin. People left with a name and a follow-up, not a stack of cards. That is why it spread.

Lena still teaches how to brief a room and hosts the annual Alivio Convention.
